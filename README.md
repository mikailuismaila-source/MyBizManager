💡 Our Vision

To build a comprehensive, intelligent and adaptable business management platform that empowers businesses of every size to manage their operations, understand their finances, achieve their goals and make better decisions.

We envision a world where business owners no longer have to depend on disconnected applications, scattered spreadsheets and manual processes to understand how their businesses are performing.

A world where every important business activity, transaction, resource, target and performance indicator can be managed from one centralized platform.

One platform to manage the business. One system to understand its performance. One vision to drive sustainable growth.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to develop an open-source business management ecosystem that brings essential business operations together.

We want to provide entrepreneurs, startups, SMEs, enterprises, retailers, service providers and organizations with the tools to:

- Plan and manage business goals.
- Record and monitor financial transactions.
- Track revenue, expenses, profit and losses.
- Manage inventory, products and services.
- Monitor customers, suppliers and relationships.
- Manage employees and business resources.
- Analyze business performance.
- Generate meaningful reports.
- Identify opportunities, risks and operational challenges.
- Make informed, data-driven decisions.

We believe business management software should be accessible, flexible, scalable and adaptable to different industries.

🌍 Why Business Manager?

Running a business involves more than selling products or providing services.

It involves managing money, people, customers, suppliers, inventory, projects, risks, targets and countless daily activities.

When these activities are managed through disconnected systems, business owners can struggle to obtain a complete picture of their operations.

Business Manager aims to address this challenge by providing a unified business management environment.

Instead of switching between multiple tools, users should be able to understand and manage their business from one place.

Our ambition is to make Business Manager more than a digital record book.

We want it to become a business intelligence companion.

Future intelligent capabilities may include:

- Identifying spending patterns.
- Highlighting unusual financial activities.
- Comparing actual results with business targets.
- Identifying profitable and underperforming products.
- Providing cash flow insights.
- Identifying operational inefficiencies.
- Generating business performance summaries.
- Supporting forecasting and scenario analysis.
- Providing contextual recommendations based on business data.

AI-assisted insights will be designed to support human decision-making, not replace business owners or professional financial judgment.

🔐 Security, Privacy and Data Ownership

Business information is valuable and must be protected.

Security and privacy will be fundamental development requirements.

Our intended security principles include:

- Secure authentication.
- Role-based access control.
- Strong password protection.
- Multi-factor authentication.
- Encryption in transit and at rest.
- Secure session management.
- Audit trails for important activities.
- Protection against unauthorized data access.
- Secure backup and recovery procedures.
- Data export and portability.
- Tenant isolation for multi-business deployments.
- Configurable data retention policies.

Financial records and important business activities must be traceable and protected against unauthorized modification.

The system will also aim to provide clear data ownership and deployment choices.

📌 Contribution Workflow

GitHub Issues and Pull Requests will serve as the foundation of our collaborative development process.

1. Explore: Review existing issues and identify areas that need improvement.
2. Apply: Comment on an issue explaining your proposed solution and relevant experience.
3. Review and Assignment: Maintainers assess the proposal and assign suitable contributors.
4. Develop: Work on the assigned task in a dedicated branch.
5. Test: Verify that the implementation works and does not introduce regressions.
6. Submit: Open a Pull Request referencing the original issue.
7. Review: Maintainers and other contributors review the implementation.
8. Merge: Approved contributions are integrated into the project.

Please wait for approval or assignment before undertaking substantial work on an issue that requires coordination.

We encourage clear communication, well-documented code, constructive feedback and responsible development.
